THE GLOWUP ROADMAP

THE
ROADMAP.

Five tools, five gentle blocks, a few minutes a day. No chemicals, no heat damage, no gimmicks — a beauty ritual for scalp, skin, lashes, and hair that compounds over months. This is the glowup way.

BLOCK 01 · SHOWER · 3 MINUTES

HAPPY HAIR STARTS AT THE SCALP

Your hair grows from the scalp, so that’s where the ritual begins. A soft silicone brush works shampoo into a real lather, lifts away buildup fingers miss, and turns every wash into a mini head-spa. Three minutes, a few times a week — the rest of your routine builds on a clean root.

  1. 01Apply shampoo as usual, then bring in the brush.
  2. 02Massage in small circles, hairline to crown.
  3. 03Rinse the brush after every shower and air dry.

BLOCK 02 · MORNING · 2 MINUTES

DE-PUFF AND SCULPT BEFORE YOUR PRODUCTS

Cold and stone work before anything you apply. A cryo ice roller calms morning puffiness and eye bags in about a minute; a sculpting gua sha glides along the jaw and neck to ease tension. Tools first, then your usual routine — they’re the perfect primer.

  1. 01Roll the ice roller in upward strokes, 60 seconds per side.
  2. 02Glide the gua sha along the jaw and up the neck.
  3. 03Follow with your own serum or moisturizer.
ICE ROLLER + GUA SHA — $21.99 →

BLOCK 03 · AS NEEDED · 10 MINUTES

SALON LASHES, ON YOUR OWN SCHEDULE

The finishing detail. A heated curler locks in a lift on even the straightest lashes — no glue, no perms — and it does the work while you stay in control. A few delicate seconds at the base, a roll upward, and the curl holds the day.

  1. 01Press the warm ceramic pad at the lash base, hold 3–5 seconds.
  2. 02Roll upward slowly; curl lasts the day.
  3. 03Store dry; wipe the pad clean between uses.
HEATED LASH CURLER — $24.99 →

BLOCK 04 · AS NEEDED · 10 MINUTES

CLUSTER LASHES, WITHOUT THE MESS

The precision helpers for the DIY cluster-lash boom. Soft crescent pads sit under the eye to shield your lower lashes while you place clusters up top — catching slips and stray glue so your set goes on clean. Tool-only: no adhesives included, use the bond you already trust.

  1. 01Press a crescent pad along the lower lash line to cover the bottom lashes.
  2. 02Apply your clusters as usual — the pad catches slips and stray glue.
  3. 03Peel the pad away once the glue sets; rinse and air-dry the pads.

BLOCK 05 · AS NEEDED · 30 SECONDS

THE EFFORTLESS FINISH: CLIP IT UP

Not every day is a wash-and-style day. Acetate claw clips pull hair up in seconds with a clean, secure hold that flatters every face — the pulled-together look that takes no time at all. The accessory that finishes the ritual.

  1. 01Twist or gather hair at the nape or crown.
  2. 02Clamp the clip closed over the twist, tines secured.
  3. 03Adjust height to your comfort — it stays put all day.

BLOCK 06 · DAILY · NON-NEGOTIABLE

THE FOUNDATION NO TOOL REPLACES

The tools compound; the base is boring and mandatory. Sleep 7–9 hours, drink water, protect your hair and skin, and stay consistent. A ritual run daily beats a spa day run rarely. Small inputs, compounded over months, are the entire secret.

  1. 01Sleep 7–9 hours. Recovery is when change happens.
  2. 02Water, SPF on skin, protective styles for hair — every day.
  3. 03Run the full ritual for 90 days before you judge it.

Consistency beats intensity. Miss a day, run it the next. The people who change are the ones still running the routine at day 90.
